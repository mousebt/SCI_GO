# Local 39-record documentation audit: required inputs

The local component cannot move from planned to completed until the following items are verified. The 39-record count is not evidence of the degree of under-reporting.

## 1. Governance

- institution/department name for internal records;
- anonymised setting description for the manuscript;
- IRB/ethics approval number, formal exemption or authorised quality-improvement determination;
- waiver of consent or consent requirement;
- data custodian and authorised access route;
- confirmation that only aggregate, de-identified results will enter the manuscript.

## 2. Study period and source system

- exact start and end dates;
- source type: paper register, incident-reporting system, pharmacovigilance system, nursing record or other;
- responsible department and who normally enters records;
- whether the source is intended to capture all suspected contrast-related events or only selected categories;
- whether parallel reporting routes exist.

Handwritten-image transcription is not part of the active workflow. The audit requires authorised manual abstraction or author-supplied aggregate coding.

## 3. Retrieval path

Record each flow count:

1. all records retrieved from the source during the study period;
2. records screened;
3. duplicates removed;
4. non-contrast events excluded;
5. events outside the study period excluded;
6. records excluded for other prespecified reasons;
7. final included suspected contrast-related event records.

Also provide:

- query terms, index categories or manual-register page range;
- drug names and spelling/brand variants used for retrieval;
- event terms or categories used for retrieval;
- duplicate definition;
- whether follow-up entries were merged with an initial event;
- reason for every exclusion category.

## 4. Inclusion criteria

Final author decision required for:

- event occurred during or after administration of an iodinated contrast medium;
- record identifies or plausibly refers to a contrast exposure;
- event was entered in the designated source within the study period;
- initial and follow-up documentation for one event counted as one record;
- age, setting or procedure restrictions, if any.

## 5. Exclusion criteria

Final author decision required for:

- non-iodinated or non-contrast products;
- events unrelated to administration;
- duplicate or follow-up-only entries;
- test/training records;
- records outside the study period;
- records with insufficient information even to establish that a contrast-related event was suspected;
- other locally applicable exclusions.

An incomplete report should not be excluded merely because required fields are missing; missingness is the outcome of the documentation audit.

## 6. Coding dictionary

For every audited field and relationship, define:

- `documented`;
- `partial_or_ambiguous`;
- `missing`;
- `not_applicable`;
- examples and edge-case rules;
- whether information elsewhere in the authorised record may be used or only the reporting form itself.

## 7. Independent review

- reviewer 1 role and training;
- reviewer 2 role and training;
- confirmation that coding is independent before comparison;
- whether all 39 records or a prespecified subset will be dual-coded;
- disagreement-resolution process;
- raw agreement;
- Cohen's kappa and confidence interval for suitable categorical items;
- no prespecified requirement that kappa must exceed 0.80.

## 8. Aggregate outputs

- documented/partial/missing/not-applicable `n/N (%)` for each field;
- explicit product-event relationship completeness;
- administration-event timeline completeness;
- event-escalation, event-management and event-outcome relationship completeness;
- number meeting verified minimum report-validity elements;
- missingness denominator for every percentage.

No local incidence or under-reporting rate should be calculated unless a separate, valid denominator and case-ascertainment design are available.
