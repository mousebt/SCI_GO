# Journal fit

Direction ID: `nursing-cross-disciplinary-incubator`

| Journal/type | Fit | Concern | Evidence to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nursing Open | Good for applied nursing, patient safety, open-access nursing research | APC; database-only manuscript must still be clearly nursing-relevant | 是否发表过 pharmacovigilance / database / patient safety informatics 类文章 |
| BMC Nursing | Good for broad nursing and care delivery studies | OA APC; methods must be transparent | 是否接受公共数据库和方法学/质量改进主题 |
| Journal of Clinical Nursing | Good nursing audience and patient-safety relevance | 难度高于低门槛 OA 期刊；需要护理理论/临床意义更强 | 近年是否有真实世界数据、AI 文书、患者安全研究 |
| International Nursing Review | Good if direction has policy/workforce/global nursing relevance | 单纯数据库信号检测可能不合适 | 是否可上升到护理政策或全球安全议题 |
| Journal of Patient Safety | Good for patient safety framing; nursing is not唯一要求 | 需要强患者安全意义，不能只是一般护理 | 是否接受 FAERS/数据库信号研究 |
| Health services / clinical informatics mixed journals | Good for AI documentation and process mining | 护理身份可能弱，需要选合适期刊 | 是否比护理期刊更适合具体题目 |

## Preliminary fit judgment

如果第一篇做“护理 + 药物警戒/患者安全”，优先选 Nursing Open / BMC Nursing / Journal of Patient Safety 这一类宽口径期刊。若题目技术性更强，可以转向 clinical informatics 或 health-services 期刊。
