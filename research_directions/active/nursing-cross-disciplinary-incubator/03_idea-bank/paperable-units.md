# Paperable units

Direction ID: `nursing-cross-disciplinary-incubator`

| Unit | Possible title | Minimum data | Minimum result | Target outlet/type | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | Pharmacovigilance signals of nursing-relevant adverse events associated with [drug/device/exposure]: a real-world FAERS study | FAERS reports, drug dictionary, MedDRA PT/SOC, onset dates if available | 阳性 SOC/PT 信号、TTO、护理监测建议 | Nursing Open / BMC Nursing / patient safety journal | best first unit |
| U2 | Nursing implications of adverse event reporting signals for [procedure/therapy] in older adults | FAERS 或 MIMIC + 年龄分层 | 老年亚组信号和护理敏感事件画像 | nursing / geriatric care journal | promising |
| U3 | Can large language models identify patient-safety risks from nursing handoff notes? A validation study | 脱敏或模拟交接班文本，人工标注金标准 | 抽取性能、漏报/误报类型、风险分类一致性 | nursing informatics / clinical informatics | later |
| U4 | Wearable sensor-derived activity and sleep patterns for postoperative nursing surveillance: a scoping review or pilot study | 公开数据或本院小样本 | 监测指标和护理结局之间的关联 | digital health / nursing journal | later |
| U5 | Climate-sensitive nursing workload during extreme heat events: a retrospective time-series study | 气象数据 + 护理工作量/急诊就诊 | 热浪与护理需求变化 | public health nursing / health services | later |

## First paperable unit recommendation

U1 is the fastest and most compatible with the current project infrastructure. It should be narrowed to one exposure family and one nursing-relevant safety question before promotion to a paper project.
