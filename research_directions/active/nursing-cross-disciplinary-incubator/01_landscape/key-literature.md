# Key literature

Direction ID: `nursing-cross-disciplinary-incubator`

## Search notes

This is a quick landscape scan for direction selection, not a final systematic search. Several current signals come from 2024 preprints or broad reviews and must be replaced or supplemented with peer-reviewed sources if the direction is promoted to a paper.

## Candidate anchor papers / trend signals

| Topic | Citation/link | Why it matters | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLMs in critical care | Stochastic Parrots or ICU Experts? Large Language Models in Critical Care Medicine: A Scoping Review. https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19256 | Shows LLMs are being explored for decision support, documentation, education and communication in high-risk clinical settings; useful for nursing documentation/handoff ideas. | preliminary; verify peer-reviewed alternatives |
| LLMs and EHR | A scoping review of using Large Language Models to investigate Electronic Health Records. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11562 | Maps common EHR + LLM tasks: extraction, summarization, classification, prediction and dialogue; directly relevant to nursing notes. | preliminary; verify |
| Multimodal AI in medicine | Navigating the landscape of multimodal AI in medicine. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03066 | Gives a broad view of image/text/time-series integration and implementation barriers; relevant if nursing data combine notes, labs and sensor records. | preliminary; verify |
| Wearable sensor health data | Functional Data Analysis on Wearable Sensor Data: A Systematic Review. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03782 | Supports the idea that high-frequency wearable data are a mature enough data type for health research, but method/data barriers remain. | preliminary; verify |

## Literature gaps to check before promotion

- 护理学期刊中是否已有“FAERS/药物警戒 + nursing implications”的同类文章；如果很少，反而可以形成定位。
- Nursing Open、Journal of Clinical Nursing、International Nursing Review、BMC Nursing 等期刊是否接受纯公共数据库/药物警戒方向。
- AI 护理文书方向需要区分“模型性能论文”和“护理质量/安全论文”，否则容易投到护理期刊时显得技术太重。
- 可穿戴方向需要确认是否有可直接获得的数据集；没有数据时不建议作为第一篇。
