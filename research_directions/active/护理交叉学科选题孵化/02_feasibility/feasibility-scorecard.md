## Red flags

- Implementation science is crowded; a generic EBP survey is unlikely to stand out.
- AI prediction can become a generic informatics paper unless nursing-sensitive variables and nursing actionability are explicit.
- Pharmacovigilance can become generic drug safety unless monitoring, education, or escalation duties are defined as nursing contributions.
- Human factors and sustainability need local access to workflow, alarm, waste, or process data.

## Fastest uncertainty-reducing step

- Ask the user which data source is actually accessible: FAERS/open data, hospital EHR/nursing assessment data, survey/interview access, alarm/workflow logs, or process/waste audit data.
