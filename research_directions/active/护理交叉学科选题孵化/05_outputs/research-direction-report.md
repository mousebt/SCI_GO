# Research direction report

Direction ID: `护理交叉学科选题孵化`

Title: 护理交叉学科选题孵化

Date: 2026-07-16

## 1. One-sentence direction

在护理实践与跨学科方法交汇处，本方向探索可由现有临床、管理、药物安全或文献数据支持的护理交叉研究问题，以形成可投稿的聚焦论文，边界是不声称尚未验证的因果机制或临床有效性。

## 2. Why this may matter

- Reader relevance: nursing managers and clinical nursing researchers need practical, evidence-grounded ways to improve safety, quality, and workforce-sensitive care.
- Clinical/research need: broad "nursing + X" topics are too loose; the publishable unit must connect a nursing-sensitive outcome or workflow to an external discipline's method.
- Possible nursing/clinical/publication value: the strongest directions can produce actionability for nursing assessment, monitoring, implementation, or workflow redesign.

## 4. Landscape and competitors

| Topic/competitor | What exists | Gap | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI nursing prediction | Many prediction papers around falls, pressure injury, and adverse events. | Nursing-sensitive variables and bedside actionability are often weakly framed. | Needs local data and model validation. |
| Nursing pharmacovigilance | Existing pharmacovigilance and adverse-event reporting studies. | Nursing monitoring/escalation implications can be made more explicit. | Must not overclaim causality from spontaneous reports. |
| Implementation science in nursing | Very large literature on EBP implementation. | Local determinant mapping can still be publishable if tied to a specific practice and framework. | High saturation; generic surveys are weak. |
| Human factors in nursing | Smaller focused literature on alarm fatigue, workload, interruptions. | Strong opportunity to connect workflow metrics with nursing response reliability. | Data access and observation burden. |
| Sustainable nursing | Growing literature on climate, waste, and sustainability in nursing. | Need measurable process-level burden and feasible change. | Measurement may be difficult. |

| Speed to manuscript | 4 | FAERS, scoping review, or retrospective datasets could move fastest. | Local data access can slow everything. |

## 6. Recommendation

Recommended decision:

- [ ] Proceed to paper
- [x] Revise direction

- [ ] Reject

Reason: the broad direction is promising, but it must be narrowed to one data source and one paperable question before promotion into a paper project.

Best first choices:

- Fastest manuscript potential: nursing-specific pharmacovigilance or AI prediction.
- Stronger interdisciplinary novelty if local data access exists: nursing + human factors.
- Best survey/interview route: implementation science, but only with a specific nursing practice and framework.

## 7. Boundaries

This direction should not claim:

- first/unique/unprecedented status without a formal literature audit;
- causality from observational or spontaneous-report data;
- clinical effectiveness without intervention/outcome evidence;
- data availability before user confirmation.
