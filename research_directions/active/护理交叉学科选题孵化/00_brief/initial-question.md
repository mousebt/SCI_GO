# Initial question

Direction ID: `护理交叉学科选题孵化`

Title: 护理交叉学科选题孵化

## Initial framing

- Problem: nursing research needs interdisciplinary questions that are specific enough to become publishable manuscripts rather than broad topic labels.
- Population/setting: nursing practice, nursing management, patient safety, medication safety, and hospital care processes.
- Possible data: EHR/nursing assessment data, FAERS or hospital adverse-event data, survey/interview data, alarm/workflow logs, process or waste audit data, and literature databases.
- Possible method: predictive modeling, pharmacovigilance disproportionality analysis, implementation-science framework analysis, human-factors workflow analysis, scoping review, or process audit.
- Possible article type: retrospective observational study, data-mining study, mixed-methods study, scoping review, or implementation study.
- Target reader: nursing researchers, clinical nursing managers, informatics researchers, pharmacovigilance researchers, and patient-safety readers.
- Boundary: do not claim novelty, causality, clinical effectiveness, or data availability without verification.

One-sentence direction:

> 在护理实践与跨学科方法交汇处，本方向探索可由现有临床、管理、药物安全或文献数据支持的护理交叉研究问题，以形成可投稿的聚焦论文，边界是不声称尚未验证的因果机制或临床有效性。
