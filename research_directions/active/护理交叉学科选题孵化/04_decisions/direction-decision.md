## Decision rationale

User and assistant agree that, given current materials, FAERS/open pharmacovigilance data plus a nursing design has publication potential, but it is a high-risk path and is not naturally favored by many journals if the nursing contribution is only added after signal detection.

The direction should therefore be revised rather than promoted directly into a paper project. The next version must make nursing contribution central: nursing monitoring, patient education, escalation, ADR reporting behavior, workflow, or nurse-led follow-up must be part of the research question, evidence structure, or result framework.

## User approval / instruction

User statement: “结合目前我们手头有的数据，以faers+护理设计是有发文可能的，只是风险比较大，不是一个大部分杂志喜欢的路径”

## Impact

- Do not abandon the FAERS/open pharmacovigilance route.
- Do not treat FAERS as the default main path.
- Classify FAERS + nursing as a publishable but high-risk backup/conditional route.
- Before promotion to a paper project, produce a risk-reduction design that strengthens nursing actionability and journal fit.
