# Promotion checklist

Before promoting a research direction into `papers/<slug>/`, confirm:

1. Exact research question.
2. Data source and access status.
3. Article type.
4. Target reader or journal direction.
5. Main evidence expected.
6. Boundary: what the paper will not claim.
7. Start mode:
   - `existing_zh_manuscript`
   - `materials_from_scratch`
   - `auto`
8. Immediate deliverable after promotion.

If any item is unknown, keep the direction in `research_directions/active/` and record the missing item in the next-action plan.
