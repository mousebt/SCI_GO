## Scope and article fit

- Target manuscript types: original nursing research and clinically relevant nursing scholarship, pending manual author-guideline verification.
- Good-fit topics: radiology nursing surveillance, adverse-event recognition, escalation preparedness, patient education, safety-sensitive nursing observation.
- Desk-reject risks: the evidence base is FAERS pharmacovigilance rather than nurse/patient primary data; no nursing intervention, nursing workflow variable, nurse sample, or nursing outcome was tested.
- Required framing changes: make nursing surveillance the research problem, present FAERS as a signal-prioritisation tool for nursing risk recognition, and explicitly avoid claims that nursing actions reduce event rates.
