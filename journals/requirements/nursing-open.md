## Scope and current metrics

- Scope: peer-reviewed open access journal covering nursing and midwifery practice, research, education, and policy.
- Official Wiley page captured on 2026-07-14 displays: CiteScore 5.9, Journal Impact Factor 3.1, acceptance rate 11%, and median submission-to-first-decision time 61 days.
- The journal explicitly warns not to submit work that is not relevant to nurses, nursing, and topics nurses are involved with and care about.

## Publication model and charges

- Fully open access. Accepted and published articles are immediately freely available.
- Standard APC displayed on the official APC page: USD 3,560 / GBP 2,230 / EUR 2,670.
- Brief Report discounted APC displayed: USD 1,246 / GBP 780 / EUR 934.
- The journal states it does not charge submission fees or page charges.
- Taxes may be added depending on jurisdiction.
- Licenses: Creative Commons options include CC BY, CC BY-NC, and CC BY-NC-ND; funder mandates may require CC BY.

## Submission system and peer review

- New submissions use Wiley Research Exchange: https://wiley.atyponrex.com/journal/NOP2
- Double-blind peer review. The manuscript must be anonymized.
- A separate title page file is required.
- ORCID is required.
- The journal accepts manuscripts previously posted as preprints, but preprints may compromise anonymity.

## Free Format first submission

- Nursing Open offers Free Format submission.
- The manuscript must be editable and include all required sections: abstract, introduction, methods, results, and conclusions.
- Figures and tables may be embedded or submitted separately; legends are required.
- References may be in any consistent style at first submission.
- Supporting information should be submitted as separate files.
- Poor English quality, unreadable tables/figures, or missing information can trigger return before review.

## Likely article type for the current FAERS paper

Primary fit: **Empirical Research Quantitative**.

- Description: quantitative research based on actual observation, experiment, or self-report.
- Limits: 8,000 words maximum; up to 10 figures/tables.
- Research articles must adhere to a relevant EQUATOR reporting checklist.
- The guidelines also mention a research methodology discussion-paper route for methodology or methodological issues based on secondary data sources, but the current paper is better framed as an empirical quantitative pharmacovigilance/nursing-surveillance study unless the argument is rebuilt around methodology itself.

## Abstract and title requirements

- Structured abstract for research articles.
- Abstract maximum: 300 words.
- No abbreviations in the abstract.
- Do not report p values, confidence intervals, or other statistical parameters in the abstract.
- Abstract headings listed by the journal include: Aim(s), Design, Keywords, Review Methods, Data Sources, Results, Conclusion, Implications for the profession and/or patient care, Impact, Reporting Method, and Patient or Public Contribution / No Patient or Public Contribution.
- Title page requires a brief informative title, maximum 20 words, with keywords where possible.
- Do not use country names or abbreviations in the title.
- Include the article type name at the end of the full article title where applicable; article type name does not count toward the 20-word title maximum.
- Short running title: less than 40 characters.

## Reference requirements

- References must not exceed the article-type limit.
- References should generally be no older than 5 years from submission date.
- Use primary rather than secondary sources where possible.
- All references must be in English.
- The journal uses APA style after formatting; Free Format allows any consistent reference style at initial submission.

## Required statements and declarations

The submission may require:

- Data availability statement.
- Funding statement.
- Conflict of interest disclosure.
- Ethics approval statement.
- Patient consent statement, if applicable.
- Permission to reproduce material from other sources, if applicable.
- Clinical trial registration, if applicable.
- Reporting checklist(s).
- Patient or public contribution statement, or an explicit statement that there was no patient or public contribution.

## Main-text structure implication

The author-guideline page lists quantitative main-text headers such as Introduction, Background, The Study, Aim(s), Methods/Methodology, Design, data sources/collection, data analysis, ethical considerations, Results, Discussion, Strengths and Limitations of the Work, Recommendations for Further Research, Conclusion, and References. The current manuscript should therefore be converted to a nursing-facing quantitative empirical article rather than a generic pharmacovigilance report.

## Key manuscript implication for the current FAERS contrast-media paper

- Nursing relevance must be central, not decorative.
- The safest positioning is: FAERS signals and time-to-onset patterns can help prioritize radiology-nursing surveillance and escalation windows.
- Avoid claiming incidence, causality, comparative drug safety, or effectiveness of nursing interventions.
- Prepare a clear “No Patient or Public Contribution” statement unless the authors can document such involvement.
- Prepare a data availability statement suitable for public FAERS data plus any analysis scripts/tables the authors are willing to share.
