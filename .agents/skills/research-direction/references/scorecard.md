# Research direction scorecard

Use 1-5 scoring:

- 1 = blocking or very weak
- 2 = possible but high risk
- 3 = workable with known gaps
- 4 = strong
- 5 = very strong / low risk

Dimensions:

| Dimension | What to check |
|---|---|
| Data availability | Whether the needed data can realistically be obtained |
| Data quality | Completeness, definitions, missingness, bias, auditability |
| Sample size plausibility | Whether events/cases are likely enough for analysis |
| Method feasibility | Whether the analysis is technically executable |
| Ethics/governance | Approval, waiver, privacy and access risk |
| Literature saturation | Whether the topic is crowded or has a usable gap; include the verified count of finished papers directly supporting the candidate direction |
| Clinical/nursing relevance | Whether the reader has a reason to care |
| Journal fit | Article type, scope and likely review risk |
| Speed to manuscript | How quickly a defensible draft can be produced |
| Overall feasibility | Integrated judgment, not an average |

Finished-paper support rule:

- Report `finished-paper support: N` for each candidate question.
- Count only verified, already completed research outputs such as published articles, accepted articles, theses/dissertations, or preprints with a full manuscript.
- Do not count protocols, editorials, comments, conference abstracts without full papers, or unchecked search hits.
- Candidates with `0` finished-paper support are exploratory. Display only the highest-feasibility exploratory candidates, capped at `min(2, floor(20% of displayed recommendations))`. If the ranked recommendation list is too small for one exploratory candidate to stay within the 20% cap, move unsupported candidates to missing evidence or future exploration instead of the ranked recommendations.

Recommendation labels:

- **Proceed**: enough data/method clarity to start a paper project.
- **Revise**: promising but needs reframing or a narrower question.
- **Park**: potentially useful but blocked by data, ethics, or timing.
- **Reject**: unlikely to be publishable or not worth the cost.
